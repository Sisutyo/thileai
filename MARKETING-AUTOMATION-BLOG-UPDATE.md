   - 20% of orders via direct online store
   - 12% higher margin vs. delivery apps

5. **AOV tăng 8–15%**
   - Bundle and upsell mechanisms
   - Triggered by segmentation and timing

6. **1.665 đơn/tháng từ tệp sở hữu**
   - (47 + 8.5) × 30 = 1,665 orders/month
   - Creates stable recurring revenue

7. **14 triệu đ lợi nhuận tăng thêm/tháng**
   - 7% margin increase on 1,665 orders
   - 1,665 × 120,000 × 0.07 ≈ 14M VND/month

8. **Tỷ suất hoàn vốn tích cực trong 1–2 chu kỳ**
   - Operating costs covered by repeat sales
   - Higher margins from direct orders

   - Shorter breakeven on promotions
